Job tracker rules (for my own application spreadsheet)

The tracker should give every application exactly one next step.

Quick context: I apply to a lot of roles, so old postings pile up fast.

If the posting is more than 30 days old, mark it stale.

Anything at one of my top companies stays active.

If it's been over 3 weeks and I haven't heard back, send a follow-up,
but only for postings that aren't stale and aren't at a top company.
For top companies I follow up myself, so the tracker shouldn't.

Otherwise, just wait.

The only facts the tracker knows are: is the posting over 30 days
old (yes/no), is it a top company (yes/no), has it been over 3 weeks
since I applied (yes/no), and have I heard back (yes/no).
